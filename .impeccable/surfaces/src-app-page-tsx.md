Scope: homepage (`/`), Persuade mode, **mobile-first** (390px is the primary frame). Audience arrives mostly from Instagram on phones. Job: make a first-time visitor believe Elixira is a physician-made luxury Ayurveda house across face, hair and body, and act (WhatsApp order/enquiry; browse collections). Constraints: no prices, sizes, claims or testimonials beyond verified data; product and campaign photography pending (owner generating; labelled image slots); no checkout. Balance: face, hair and body get equal standing; hair must not dominate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a short film that follows each formula from its herb to the bottle, made by one physician in small batches; it refuses the category default of promo carousels, countdown banners and rows of equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep forest full-screen stills that own the viewport, warm ivory and beige interludes, gold only as hairline and the logo's own metal; Cormorant Garamond light at poster scale over imagery, Manrope for working text; square-cut images, 1px gold rules, 2px-radius controls, photographic scrims only; no shadows, no decorative gradients.

STORY: The visitor meets the gold seal and "Glow naturally. Heal deeply.", then watches five chapters (saffron to Kumkumadi Face Serum, bhringraj to Herbal Hair Pack, sandalwood to Sandalwood Gel, black seed to Black Seed Hair Oil, neem and tulsi to Neem & Tulsi Soap), browses every collection, meets the doctor, and messages to order from a dock that never leaves the thumb.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On a 390x844 phone: full-bleed forest stage (portrait campaign still H2 when it exists; until then the real circular seal in gold orbit rings), headline lower third at poster scale, one sentence, "Shop the collection" primary; a fixed bottom dock carries Shop and WhatsApp. Desktop keeps the same story with the headline lower-left and the seal or H1 still at the right.

FORM: Herb to Bottle, structure 6 on my ordered list, dealt as the lead by the roll and locked by the owner. seed:7ecfaf584e11f3c4. Signature interaction: each chapter is a pinned full-screen scene where the herb still scrubs into the product still as the product develops upward out of the herb (a continuous emergence, raised from the declined darkroom challenger), chapters flowing on one continuous forest surface (raised from the declined ebru challenger), with a 1-to-5 progress rail.
